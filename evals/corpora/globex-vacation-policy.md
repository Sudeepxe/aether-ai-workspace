# Globex Corp Vacation Policy

Globex full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) for
every month of continuous employment, up to a maximum of 18 days per
calendar year.

## Accrual

Accrual begins on an employee's start date and is credited at the end
of each completed month. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated
basis matching their scheduled hours.

## Rollover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be rolled over into the following calendar
year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st unless
local law requires otherwise.
