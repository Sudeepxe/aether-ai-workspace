# Acme Corp Refund Policy

Acme Corp offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all annual
subscriptions purchased directly from acme.example.

## Refund process

Customers may request a refund by contacting support within the 30-day
window. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5
business days of approval.

## Exclusions

Custom enterprise contracts negotiated outside the standard subscription
flow are not covered by this policy and are instead governed by their
individual service agreements.
