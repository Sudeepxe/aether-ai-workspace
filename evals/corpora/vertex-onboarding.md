# Vertex Labs New Hire Onboarding

## First day

On their first day, a new Vertex employee completes IT setup (laptop
provisioning, account creation), signs compliance paperwork with HR,
and has a welcome lunch with their team.

## First week

During the first week, new hires meet one-on-one with their manager
and complete the mandatory security-awareness training module.

On day two specifically, new hires meet with a peer "buddy" assigned to
help them navigate their first month.
