# Zylonix Support Policy

Zylonix offers a 45-day money-back guarantee on all annual subscriptions
purchased through the Zylonix billing portal.

## Guarantee window

The 45-day window is measured from the original purchase date, not from
the date of first use. Guarantee eligibility applies only to the
enterprise plan.

## Refund claims

To claim a refund under the guarantee, contact support with your order
number within the 45-day window. Approved claims are refunded to the
original payment method within 7 business days.
